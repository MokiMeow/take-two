## Task
1. **Research real disruptions.** Use production guides, AD and line-producer advice, creator forums and filmmaker posts on Reddit (r/Filmmakers, r/videography, r/NewTubers), and wedding videographer communities. List the disruptions that really happen and how often, citing URLs. Cover at least:

   - Cast: sick, late, quits, availability changes.
   - Weather and light: rain, golden hour missed, heat.
   - Equipment: failure, missing batteries or cards.
   - Script: rewrites, the director changes a scene the night before, a client asks for changes (creator and brand shoots).
   - Schedule: overtime, scenes pushed to another day, two changes at once.
   - Crew: someone doesn't confirm, works from the old call sheet, a new crew member joins mid-production.
   - Money and permissions: client approval, a budget cap.
2. **Run each scenario through the agreed design.** For each, give:

   - **Value:** how much it matters to real crews.
3. **Messy human and system cases.** Cover at least:
   - the director doesn't answer before the deadline;
   - the director rejects both options;

   Say exactly how the design handles each, with Switch source references where it relies on a primitive.
