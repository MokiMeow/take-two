# Research

## What the evidence shows
- **Shoot plans change, and outdated call sheets cause confusion.** StudioBinder's production guides describe successive call-sheet versions, department sign-off and confirming receipt as normal work:
  - https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/production-call-sheet/
  - https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/how-call-sheets-are-approved-on-features-and-television/
- **Real accounts gathered in debate round 3** (see `debate/ROUND3-SOL.md` for the links): a permit refused after months of verbal approval; a student film whose actors cancelled at the last minute five times; a wedding shoot hit by hail; cameras failing mid-wedding; a client who turned one interview into four the day before.

## What is not verified
- **How often** these disruptions happen. No hard numbers were found.
- Whether small crews would adopt a Discord or Mattermost tool (many coordinate on WhatsApp, which Switch doesn't bridge).
- Time saved. Not measured.

## The claim we make
Only this: **a consequential human decision reaches everyone as one consistent, current plan, and anyone who hasn't seen it is visible.**
